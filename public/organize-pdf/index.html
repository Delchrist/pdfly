Questions

Can I undo a deletion?

Yes. Deleted pages stay greyed out until you save; click restore to bring one back.

Is PDFly free?

Yes. Merge, split, compress, organize, rotate and convert are free. PDFly Pro unlocks watermarks, page numbers, password protection, signatures, batch processing and files up to 1 GB.

Are my files safe?

Your files are processed entirely inside your browser. They are never uploaded to our servers, so nobody else can see them.
